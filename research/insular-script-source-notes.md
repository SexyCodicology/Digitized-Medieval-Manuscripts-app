# Insular Scripts: editorial source locations

These locations supported the article review on 30 October 2026. They are
retained here as an editorial reference after removing the reader-facing
source checklist. The article keeps its MLA citations, Works Cited, and image
credits. Image provenance and reuse terms are recorded separately in
[insular-script-image-sources.md](insular-script-image-sources.md).

## Passage locations

The following locations identify the passages and manuscript reproductions
used above:

- Stokes: “Introduction/Overview”; the opening paragraph of “Development”;
  the opening paragraph of “Characteristics and Letterforms”; and plates
  15.1–15.2 with their transcriptions and commentary.
- *Ad fontes*, “Insular Scripts”: the image and caption for Schaffhausen,
  Stadtbibliothek, Gen. 1, p. 4; “Abbreviations”; and “Later development”.
- Gamper and Marti: “Seiteneinrichtung” (page layout) in the online description
  of Schaffhausen, Stadtbibliothek, Gen. 1. The printed description occupies
  pages 67–68 of their catalogue; the online text includes additions from 2008.
- *Earlier Latin Manuscripts*, CLA II.273: “Script Commentary”, including
  the observation about fol. 116 of the Book of Durrow.
- “About the Book of Kells”: the opening two substantive paragraphs.
- “SFI-IRC Pathway Project”: the opening two substantive paragraphs, with
  twentieth-century typography discussed in the first and the research project
  in the second.
- “Cotton MS Nero D IV”: “Contents” and “Provenance”, particularly the
  description and transcription of Aldred's colophon on fol. 259r.
