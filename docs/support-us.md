# Support Us

DMMapp is a labor of love — free, open, and built by people who simply enjoy tracking down digitized manuscripts. If you'd like to support the project, here are two ways to do it.

## Patreon

The best way to support us is by becoming a patron. Even a small monthly pledge helps us keep researching, adding new libraries, and building new features.

[Become a Patron](https://www.patreon.com/c/SexyCodicology){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## Merchandise

Prefer to get something in return? Our [RedBubble store](store.md) sells posters and merchandise featuring illuminated manuscript details.

[Visit the Store](https://sexycodicology.redbubble.com){ .md-button }

## Why support Sexy Codicology?

Sexy Codicology is a product of passion and hard work — our aim is to share medieval manuscripts and grow interest in the subject. We try to create resources that are useful to researchers around the world, and constantly look for new ways to bring these hundred-year-old manuscripts into the digital world.

We do all this because we love it. It's our passion. If you think we're worth it, supporting us helps us keep researching and building new projects.
