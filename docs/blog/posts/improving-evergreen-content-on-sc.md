# Improving evergreen content on SC

We are rewriting and improving some of our pages. The first one to be edited was the "Codicology" page that we have linked here.
It's not perfect yet: it misses some content, and the style is a bit wonky...  But we'll keep working on it! Once that is done, we'll move to other cornerstone pages on the Sexy Codicology website.
We have also started using MLA citation format for our references on our posts.
Although this will very often link to locked journals, and many readers will not be able to read the full texts, we find it important to cite our sources whenever we write these pages. Students, researchers, or enthusiasts can then find the texts we use and gain access via their university or library.
We'll do our best find OA alternatives to link to.
The aim is to have citations site-wide: pages and blog posts, Over time, all our content will be properly referenced.
Like it should have been from the beginning, but we lacked the proper tools (and knowledge!) to do so :)
