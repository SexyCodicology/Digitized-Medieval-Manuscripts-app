# DMMapp upgrade - status update

So, late on schedule we are upgrading the DMMapp; as we have been posting in the past weeks, we are off to a new server and to HTTPS.
The process started a couple of hours ago this Tuesday morning and now we are waiting for the Internet to take notice of the changes. We are also signaling all the services that we would like the website always to load over HTTPS rather than the less secure HTTP.
In the meanwhile, you will most possibly see a error page instead of the DMMapp. This is normal and expected.
**It will take from 24 to 48 hours until everything will be back to normal and the DMMapp will be usable again.**
This will vary per region. Some areas in the world will be able to access the DMMapp earlier than others; we have no power over this.
As always, all the [DMMapp data is always accessible via GitHub](https://github.com/SexyCodicology/DMMapp/blob/master/js/data.json). If you are desperatly searching for a resource or some manuscripts from a specific library: check and search our code!
We are sorry for the inconvenience, but it is something that is essential for this project!
Thank you for your understanding, and see you on the HTTPS side!
