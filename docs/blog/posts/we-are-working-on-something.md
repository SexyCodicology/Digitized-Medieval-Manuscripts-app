# We are working on something...

Yep! It involves, as always, code, data, and obviously, digitized medieval manuscripts :) Stay tuned as we develop the idea and release it into the wild. Soon.
We have also started doing some interesting research on the world of digitized material, its current status, and what could be done to improve the world of manuscripts. It's very dry and boring material at the moment, but we are going to spice it up for the blog at a letter stage.
Speaking of research: we have started putting together the material for our presentation next month in Belgium. Interesting stuff in our opinion!
As always, once we are done, you will be able to read and download everything from sexycodicology.net! See you there.
