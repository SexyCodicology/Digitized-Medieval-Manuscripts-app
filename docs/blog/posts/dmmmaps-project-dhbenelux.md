# The DMMmaps Project at DHBenelux 2014

As you might have read on our [Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/SexyCodicology), [Twitter](https://twitter.com/SexyCodicology), Tumblr, or basically anywhere our social media empire stretches, the Sexy Codicology team was at the Digital Humanities Benelux Conference (or DH Benelux) 2014: "A conference that showcases the state of the art in digital humanities, the most recent development in humanities research.", organized by the Huygens Institute for the History of the Netherlands and the National Library of the Netherlands (Koninklijke Bibliotheek).
Useless to say, we are deeply honored and incredibly proud to have had the possibility to present the DMMmaps project to various experts and like-minded people from around the world.
If you couldn't be there, but you are interested to know what I have said during my part of the speech at the conference, I have posted it all on Medium. **In this I explain how the DMMmaps project works, what services we use, how we make them interact.**
I also explained the issues and the limits that the project faces (economical and technical ones).
Please feel free to comment here or directly on Medium. Any suggestion on how to improve the project is very welcome!
