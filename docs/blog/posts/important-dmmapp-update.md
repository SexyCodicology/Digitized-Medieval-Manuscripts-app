# Important DMMapp update!

Dear all,
We are about to update the DMMapp. Since we know that quite some users count on the DMMapp to help them in their courses and studies, we find it important to communicate some updates that we have lined up, and what disruptions can be expected.
**What is going to change?**

<!-- more -->

- We are upgrading the **DMMapp to a secure HTTPS connection**.
- We are transferring the website to a **faster server**.
- We have migrated **all the post that used to be on the DMMapp website to the Sexy Codicology blog.**

**"I need the DMMapp! How long will it be offline?**
The DMMapp might be unreachable for a few hours (or a day... or two. Depends how good we are at messing things up.)
**The update will take place on Friday 28 October.** We'll keep you posted on Twitter ([@SexyCodicology](https://twitter.com/sexycodicology)) and on Facebook ([fb.com/SexyCodicology](https://www.facebook.com/SexyCodicology/))
A big thank you to our supporters here on Patreon who are making this upgrade possible!
