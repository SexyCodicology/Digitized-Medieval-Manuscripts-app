# The Codicologist's Store

Welcome to the Sexy Codicology Store! Thanks to the wonderful Public Domain licenses under which so many libraries release their digitized manuscripts, we've taken some of the most beautiful manuscript leaves and turned them into posters and merchandise, so you can bring a bit of illuminated manuscript into your home or office. (The real manuscripts belong in libraries.)

If you want to profess your love for medieval manuscripts to the outside world, or simply give us a hand, this is the place.

[Visit the RedBubble Store](https://sexycodicology.redbubble.com){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
